## Project Objective
To contribute to the succses of a business by utilizing data analysis techniques, specifically focusing on time series analysis,to provide valuable insights and accurate sales forecasting.

## Project Title
Super Store Sales Performance & Strategic Insights Dashboard.
An interactive dashboard analyzing sales performance, year-over-year trends, and profitability across segments, categories, and regions. Built to help identify revenue drivers and uncover strategic growth opportunities.

## Short Description
This dashboard analyzes Super Store sales across segments, categories, and regions, highlighting year-over-year trends and profitability. Its purpose is to identify revenue drivers—such as the dominant Consumer segment and Office Supplies category—and uncover growth opportunities in underperforming areas like the South region. By visualizing regional disparities and volatile YoY performance, it enables data-driven decisions to stabilize sales, improve margins, and optimize strategic focus.

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **Consumer** segment drives **44%** of total sales.
- **Office Supplies** is the top category, with **Phones**, **Chairs**, and **Binders** as leading sub‑categories.
- **West region** outperforms others; the **South** region shows untapped potential.
- **YoY performance** is volatile, with swings of **-43%** to **+35%**, signaling a need for better demand forecasting.
